Our visits

What each visit is for

Pick the one closest to why you are coming. If you are not sure, ask us when you get in touch.

General consultations

A visit to talk through a health concern with the doctor. It suits you when something specific is on your mind.

Ask: which details about my concern are most useful to share?

Preventive-care visits

A visit centred on staying well rather than on a single problem. Ask the clinic what a visit covers before you come.

Ask: what would help me look after my health day to day?

Chronic-condition follow-up

Return visits to review a long-term condition you already live with, so your questions have a regular place to be asked.

Ask: how often should I come back, and what should I bring?

Questions people ask first

Which visit should I choose?

If you have something specific to discuss, ask about a general consultation. For a long-term condition you already have, ask about chronic-condition follow-up. For a visit centred on staying well, ask about preventive-care visits.

What if it is an emergency?

In a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a clinic visit.

How do I find the clinic?
