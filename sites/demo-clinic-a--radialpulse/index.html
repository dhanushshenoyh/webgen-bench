Our services

What we look after, in three kinds of visit

Choose the visit closest to what you want to talk about. If you are not sure, say so when you get in touch and we will point you to the right one.

01

General consultations

A visit to talk through a health concern with a doctor: something new, something that keeps coming back, or a question you want answered.

Worth asking: Which concern should we start with?

02

Preventive-care visits

A visit focused on staying well rather than treating a problem. It is a chance to talk about your health and how to look after it.

Worth asking: Which parts of my health would be useful to talk about?

03

Chronic-condition follow-up

Ongoing visits for a long-term condition you already live with, so you can go over how things are going and what to discuss next.

Worth asking: What would you like to review about my condition?

    What to discuss

  • 02

    Consider making a list of any medicines you take now, so it is easy to share.

  • 03

    Reports and scans
