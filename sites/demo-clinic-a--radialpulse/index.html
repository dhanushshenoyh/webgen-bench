Our care

Three ways to see the doctor

Pick the visit that matches why you are coming in. Each one comes with questions you can take along.

  • General consultations

    A visit to talk with the doctor about a health concern, new or ongoing, and decide what to do next.

  • You might ask

    • What should I note down about how I have been feeling?
    • Which of my questions should we cover first?

  • Preventive-care visits

    Visits for looking after your health before problems arise, so you can plan ahead with the doctor.

  • You might ask

    • Which parts of my health would be useful to review?
    • What would a sensible plan for future visits look like?

  • Chronic-condition follow-up

    Return visits to review a long-term condition you already live with and keep your care joined up.

  • You might ask

    • How often should we review my condition together?
    • Which of my current medicines should we go over?

One clinic for the questions you ask once and the ones you keep asking

Before your visit

Four small things that can help

These are suggestions, not requirements. Bring what you have.

  1. 01

    Questions to ask

    Jot down what you want to discuss, in your own words, so nothing is forgotten.

  2. 02

    Current medicines

    Make a list of the medicines you take now, so the doctor sees the full picture.

  3. 03

    Earlier reports or scans

    If you have previous reports or scans, you may want to bring them along.

  4. 04

    How to reach us

    Check the Contact page for how to find and reach the clinic.
