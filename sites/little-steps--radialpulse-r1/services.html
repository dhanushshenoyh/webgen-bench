Services

Services for babies, children and teens

Here is what each visit means in plain words, plus a few questions you could bring with you.

  • Newborn and well-baby checks
  • Vaccinations
  • Growth and nutrition advice
  • Fever and illness visits
  • Adolescent health

Check-ups, vaccines and nutrition

Three planned visits that follow a child's growth.

01

Newborn and well-baby checks

Visits in a baby's first year that look at growth, feeding and development.

Questions you could ask

  • What will you look at during each visit in the first year?
  • Can we talk through how feeding is going?
02

Vaccinations

Routine childhood immunisations, given on the national schedule.

Questions you could ask

  • Which vaccines is my child due for next?
  • Which earlier vaccination records should I bring?
03

Growth and nutrition advice

A review of your child's height, weight and diet with our nutritionist, Ms. Neha Kulkarni.

Questions you could ask

  • How has my child's height and weight changed over time?
  • Is there anything in our everyday meals worth discussing?

Illness and teenage visits

For a child who is unwell, and for a teenager who wants a check-up.

04

Fever and illness visits

Same-day visits for common childhood illnesses.

Questions you could ask

  • When is it best to bring my child in?
  • What should I tell you about how the illness started?
05

Adolescent health

Check-ups and guidance for teenagers.

Questions you could ask

  • What would my teenager like to talk about at a check-up?
  • How can we prepare for the visit together?

Quick answers

The basics about visiting Little Steps Children's Clinic.

Who can visit the clinic?

We care for newborns, children and teenagers.

Can my child be seen on the same day?

Yes, for common childhood illnesses we offer same-day fever and illness visits.

When is the clinic open?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00–20:00. We are closed on Sunday.

How much does a visit cost?

Fees are not published on this site. Please ask the clinic about the visit you have in mind.

Is there a phone number?

A phone number will be added here soon. Until then, our address and opening hours are on the contact page.

Ready to visit?

The contact page has our address, opening hours and a short list of things you might bring.
